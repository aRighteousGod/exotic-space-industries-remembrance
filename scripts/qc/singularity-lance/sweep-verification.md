# Synchronized sweep, violet/cyan lighting, and compact status

Implementation reference: [Singularity Lance mechanics and maintenance](../../../docs/singularity-lance.md).
Machine-readable evidence is generated by `analyze-sweep.py` into `sweep-results.json`.
Raw engine logs and source snapshots remain in ignored `.factorio-qc/sweep` and
`.factorio-qc/cu/l/sweep*` directories. Final candidate runs use the `sweep2-`
prefix, including the moving same-target lock and reduced scheduling overhead.
No isolated graphical fixture is required;
gameplay and native tooltip acceptance are the user's manual review.

## Scope and invariants

- Schema 13: payment at firing; contact at +8 ticks; first collapse at +38; echo
  at +68. All deadlines and coefficients are prepaid. The target determines its
  contact position at arrival; subsequent blasts stay there.
- Per-lance contact FIFO and shared Wound contexts preserve paid order through
  bursts, research/ownership changes, source removal, and save/load. Schema 11
  and 12 migrate in place; old paid collapses retain their original semantics.
- Native beams use endpoint setters and retain their source-opening animations,
  crystal origin, saturated material, and original impact bloom. No more than
  one main beam plus three incision extensions exist per lance.
- Beam ground masks use warm violet RGB(.72,.35,1), at exactly one quarter of
  their previous effective intensity. The five-tick contact flash is ice cyan;
  finite cyan afterglow uses the existing preset duration, size, and intensity.
- The native status is one compact baseline-DPS value. Exact conditional mechanics
  remain in static tooltip rows and force-current Informatron. Seven locale pairs
  are synchronized, preserving existing lore and accepted Japanese terminology.
- Native targeting, power, firing cadence, quality range, damage values, research
  identities, prerequisites, science sets, and automatic pricing are preserved.
  Every packet rechecks protection. Decoration never authorizes or suppresses damage.

## Validation

The `sweep2-verified-*` runs cover Standard, Lean, Cinematic, Maximal, Unbounded,
no scaling, flattening, and both no scaling/flattening. Each uses the enabled
frozen compatibility profile and final data-stage assertions. The final series
passed **4,946 assertions**: 4,877 across eight mechanics configurations, plus
69 across the four actual-save reload runs. Coverage includes:

- No damage before contact; moving-target endpoint tracking; native endpoint-handle
  reuse; first acquisition; compressed FIFO bursts; and exact 8/38/68 deadlines.
- Wound ramp, zero damage, retargeting, exact timeout, detached research/ownership
  contexts, and presentation migration before a first successful contact.
- Incision/collapse caps, deterministic selection, central precedence, large and
  rotated targets, all directions, quality and native bounding-box boundaries.
- Current circular blast eligibility, core/outer exclusivity, primary escape,
  fixed centers, and protection changes between packets.
- Source destruction, source-force changes, primary surface transfer, surface
  deletion, real force merging, and separate damage calls under resistance.
- Native energy payment, ordinary/scripted research, resets/reversal, visual
  overload, finite native flash/afterglow lifetime, and idle target-read absence.
- Bounded status values, saved-label refresh without research changes, and
  seven-language key/placeholder/UTF-8 parity.

Actual schema-11 and schema-12 counter-seven saves were loaded by the new module.
Their seven outstanding collapses kept their original damage, primary eligibility,
timing, and single-impact behavior. New schema-13 saves passed counter-seven reload
and eighth-shot-in-flight reload, preserving shared Wound context and linked pulses.

Wrapper checks: preflight passed all syntax/reference/encoding/locale/asset/version
checks, with two unrelated module-header warnings. `qc-fast` and `qc-runtime`
completed with existing compatibility/reference warnings and no errors.
`qc-assets` completed without warnings or errors. Initial preflight cache failures
were environmental: protected `.codex` bytecode output, followed by Windows path
length. Redirecting Python cache output to an extended-length staging path allowed
the unmodified wrapper to complete.

## Reproduction

Use Factorio 2.0.77 and a fresh `RunName` for each command. Frozen source snapshots
keep unrelated concurrent development out of the matched comparison.
The ignored snapshot paths below are retained local evidence inputs, not files
present in a fresh clone. Future comparisons must capture their own before/after
snapshots and fixed dependency profile; the generated report records source hashes.

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/invoke-singularity-lance-qc.ps1 -Mode mechanics -CurrentSource -BaselineSource .factorio-qc/sweep/after -RunName sweep-example-standard -Fidelity standard -Ticks 340 -Runs 1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/invoke-singularity-lance-qc.ps1 -Mode save -CurrentSource -BaselineSource .factorio-qc/sweep/after -RunName sweep-example-save -InFlight
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/invoke-singularity-lance-qc.ps1 -Mode reload -CurrentSource -BaselineSource .factorio-qc/sweep/after -RunName sweep-example-reload -InFlight -SaveInput .factorio-qc/cu/l/sweep-example-save/saves/lance-seven.zip -Ticks 60 -Runs 1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/invoke-singularity-lance-qc.ps1 -Mode benchmark -Scene dense -CurrentSource -BaselineSource .factorio-qc/sweep/after -RunName sweep-example-dense -Ticks 600 -Runs 5 -NoCounters
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/invoke-singularity-lance-qc.ps1 -Mode benchmark -Scene dense -CurrentSource -BaselineSource .factorio-qc/sweep/after -RunName sweep-example-profile -Ticks 600 -Runs 1 -Profile
python -B scripts/qc/singularity-lance/analyze-sweep.py
```

For baseline benchmarks select `.factorio-qc/sweep/before` and
`-FixtureSource .factorio-qc/sweep/fixture-before`; do not use the historical
pre-upgrade `-Baseline` mode. Both sides here have the Full Hybrid upgrades.
Without `-InFlight`, save/reload preserves counter seven with seven queued pulses.
The installed enabled-mod profile is frozen; this does not claim every possible
optional-mod combination or multiplayer determinism has been tested.

## Measurement method and limitations

Each whole-engine case uses map seed 410728 and 600 ticks. One complete warmup run
is discarded, then five measured run means are retained with median/min/max.
Profiler runs are separate, single-run 600-tick fixtures. Phase statistics cover
ticks 120–599, zero-filled for inactive ticks. Counters reset after tick 120 work
and the final snapshot is at 600. These windows differ by one tick at either end.

The direct case uses one lance at artificial 60 Hz. Dense and diagonal also inject
artificial paid callbacks; normal-power uses native firing and the shipped power
contract. Idle/research have 96 registered lances; dense has 96 lances and 960 targets.
Population assertions ensure damage does not reduce query populations. Headless
measurements omit GPU cost and include other enabled mods, engine work and fixture
overhead. The host is shared with other development; controls and repeated-run
variation matter, and these numbers are not isolated hardware performance claims.

`shot + update-total` is total measured lance work. Contact, incision, mechanics,
sweep, lights, impact cues, and decoration are nested phases; do not sum them all.
Phase timings include synchronous native API work and callbacks invoked by those
calls; they are attribution timers, not a count of this module's Lua instructions.
The dense 1 ms p95 objective was already unmet before this restoration and is not
assumed achieved by passing correctness fixtures.

At artificial 60 Hz, a newly promoted contact is already due next tick. Thus steady
direct-only stress executes no intermediate interpolation steps: its added work
is mainly contact snapshots/FIFO/buckets, lifecycle checks, and two finite native
light replacements per contact. Normal-power acquisitions do perform intermediate
target tracking. The contact-light profiler makes this distinction measurable.

The UPS review removed repeated generic type probes from trusted stored
LuaEntity-or-nil paths, while retaining validity checks after damage callbacks.
Ordinary exact-deadline work now takes the shared scheduler's exact bucket;
overdue work still uses its ordered catch-up helper. No parallel scheduler,
global target polling, damage merging, or light throttling was introduced.

See [the generated measurement tables](sweep-performance.md) and
[complete numerical evidence](sweep-results.json) for run ranges and phase costs.

## Measured outcome

The seven whole-engine comparisons retain five measured runs each. Native-power
combat measured **4.674 ms/update**, versus **4.814 ms** before; their run ranges
overlap. Diagonal stress measured **35.182 ms**, versus **33.535 ms**, also within
overlapping ranges. Dense stress measured **95.420 ms**, versus **103.263 ms**
(-7.60%). The no-lance control shifted between sessions, so these are observed
comparisons on a shared host, not a general hardware speedup claim.

Direct-only stress exceeded the 5% investigation threshold: **0.481 to 0.545 ms**
(+13.31%). A nearby repeated pair reproduced it at **0.490 to 0.557 ms** (+13.67%).
The nearby no-lance control moved only **0.386 to 0.397 ms** (+2.85%), so the direct
increase is larger than that control shift; no exact host-noise subtraction is assumed.
Lance-attributed direct work rose from **0.0767 ms mean / 0.1018 ms p95** to
**0.1332 ms mean / 0.1656 ms p95**. This is a real measured cost to retain in the
acceptance record, not a performance pass inferred from correctness.

The direct profile averages **0.0209 ms** in shot admission, **0.0692 ms** in
contact resolution, **0.0038 ms** in sweep bookkeeping, and **0.0147 ms** in contact
lights. These nested phase values explain where work occurs; they are not an
additive causal decomposition of the increase. Its 480 contacts produce 960 finite
light effects and no intermediate interpolation steps. By contrast, the native-power
profile records **9,002 intermediate sweep steps**, averaging **0.1384 ms/update**
for the sweep phase across its 96 lances. Contact lighting there averages **0.0197 ms**.

The artificial dense fixture's lance-attributed **129.7045 ms p95** remains far
above the **1 ms objective**. Its mean is **90.4186 ms/update**. Major phase means
are incision selection **27.1687 ms**, incision damage **13.4041 ms**, first collapse
**29.9719 ms**, and echo **3.6786 ms**. Core shot/impact presentation averages
**5.3239 ms**, contact lighting **0.7952 ms**, sweep bookkeeping **0.2336 ms**, and
optional decoration **0.1614 ms**. Selection, damage and pulses dominate this
60-shot-per-second-per-lance stress; reducing optional decoration alone cannot
close that gap. All paid packets remain intact.

No-lance, idle, and unrelated-research profiles have empty lance counters and zero
attributed lance work in their measurement windows. The exact-ID dispatcher and
dedicated unrelated-effect fixture also pass. The restored sweep introduces no
idle target reads, Wound scans, or unrelated-research entity scans.

## Manual graphical acceptance

Use normal gameplay zoom in daylight and darkness, Lean and Standard. Inspect each
research level separately, then the combined weapon and a dense 96-lance layout.

- Main beam starts at the raised crystal. The regular, Axial, and Testament
  source-opening animation transitions cleanly into the matching beam material.
- First acquisition grows outward; subsequent attacks sweep continuously from
  the last endpoint. Same-target attacks stay locked. Rapid retargeting reaches
  each paid contact without a growing backlog or old beam segments accumulating.
- Damage agrees with visible contact. Forks appear at contact and meet at ground
  level; no height-offset disconnect, clipped endpoints, or missing impact bloom.
- Collapse warnings show their full 30-tick phase and readable core/outer bounds.
  Testament has the stronger first impact, then a second contraction and terminal
  cross exactly 30 ticks later. Blasts remain at contact when the enemy moves away.
- Ground wash is subdued warm violet, with a distinct short ice-cyan contact flash
  and cyan afterglow. Verify terrain illumination, not just bright beam sprites.
- Wound's three bands remain recognizable on moving enemies, worms, and spawners;
  the full halo means +100%. Retargeting/expiry/protection removes the old mark.
- Lean retains all mechanical cues. Standard decoration and the 96-lance scene
  must remain readable without excessive light or screen-filling trails.
- Native tooltip: baseline and all research levels, particularly Legendary/full
  upgrades; ordinary/enlarged UI scale; all seven locales. The compact status must
  fit one line, its green diode must align, and the lore's final word must remain
  above the separator. The screenshot's stray “line.” needs explicit inspection;
  unchanged lore plus a shorter status is not proof of a repaired native layout.
- Static rows stay readable; Informatron still shows exact force-adjusted values,
  research states and conditional details removed from the diode row.

No graphical acceptance is claimed from headless success. No release deployment,
commit, or push is part of this change. Unrelated dirty work is preserved.
