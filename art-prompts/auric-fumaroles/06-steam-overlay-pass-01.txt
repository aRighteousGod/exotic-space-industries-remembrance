Create a single Factorio-ready top-down effect sprite concept for the steam overlay of an auric precipitation fumarole on Vulcanus.

This asset is the vapor layer only. It will sit above a separate basalt vent/crack base sprite.

Requirements:
- transparent background
- one centered asset only
- strict top-down or near-top-down game asset read
- readable silhouette at Factorio gameplay scale
- no rock, no ground plate, no crater rim, no surrounding terrain, no UI, no text
- the plume should emerge from a narrow central throat area and stay spatially compact
- pale superheated steam outer body, ash-gray contamination, faint muted auric-gold inner shimmer near the throat
- feels pressurized, dangerous, mineral-bearing, and volcanic rather than like soft fantasy mist
- irregular, broken plume shape with a few strong directional curls rather than a big round cloud
- avoid giant smoke billows, avoid photoreal fog, avoid purple or blue energy, avoid clean magical swirls

Composition target:
Think of this as a reusable overlay that could be animated later. The center should be strongest, while the outer wisps should thin quickly so the base vent and Vulcanus tiles remain readable.

Style target:
Austere industrial occult geology. Harsh hot breath, not decorative steam.
