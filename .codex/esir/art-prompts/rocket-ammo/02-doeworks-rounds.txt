Doeworks rocket ammo art prompts for Exotic Space Industries: Remembrance.

The Doeworks line should feel extremely potent inside a narrow niche. The art should reinforce weight, restraint, and brutal payoff. Fixed cone logic is part of the identity.

Prompt goals:
- Siege round: oversized, armored, blunt, built for breaching
- Corrosive round: grim containment, acid-carrying shell, industrial hazard markings
- Cryo round: insulated shell, cold conduit markings, frozen containment hardware

Keep the forms readable from a top-down game camera. The visual language should feel heavier than the hand rockets, not just larger.
