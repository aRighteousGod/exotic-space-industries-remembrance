Rocket ordnance effects for Exotic Space Industries: Remembrance.

Generate separate effect sheets for siege, corrosive, cryo, and atomic families. The language should feel industrial and severe, not glossy or cartoonish.

Effect targets:
- Siege explosion: heavy impact burst, dust ring, shrapnel punch, short-lived smoke
- Corrosive explosion: acid spray, toxic vapor, dark green or yellow-brown mist, lingering corrosive smear
- Cryo explosion: cold flash, frost vapor, pale shard burst, freezing haze
- Atomic explosion: brutal containment failure, bright core, hot ring, heavy fallout bloom
- Corrosive sticker: readable hazard stain, persistent and ugly
- Cryo sticker: thin frost overlay, lingering control signal

Keep motion readable in combat. Each family should look distinct from vanilla stock effects at a glance.
